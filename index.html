    How to use VeraHire
    A complete walkthrough — bookmark this tab or share your screen when showing someone how it works.
    What is VeraHire?

    VeraHire is an AI-powered candidate fraud screener built by Radhika Pothuraju, a Senior Technical Recruiter with 10+ years of experience in IT and corporate staffing. Every fraud signal in this tool comes from real patterns observed in the field — fabricated resumes, proxy interviews, AI-generated applications, visa misrepresentation, and more.

    VeraHire uses Claude AI (Anthropic) to analyze resumes and documents against 10 fraud dimensions, then generates targeted interview questions to verify findings in real conversations.

    Step-by-step workflow
    1
    Receive a resume from a candidate
    When a candidate applies or a vendor submits a profile, copy the full resume text. Also copy the job description you're hiring for — this is what makes VeraHire detect JD fabrication, the most common fraud pattern.
    2
    Run the fraud screen
    Paste the resume and JD into the Screen Candidate tab. Toggle which of the 10 fraud signals you want checked. Click Run Full Fraud Screen — results arrive in about 20 seconds.
    3
    Read the risk score and dimension breakdown
    You get a 0–100 risk score and a HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW verdict. Each of the 10 fraud dimensions shows its own score with specific flags — not generic observations, but exact things found in that resume. Score above 60 = dig deeper before proceeding.
    4
    Use the interview probes
    VeraHire generates 6 targeted questions designed to expose the specific fraud signals it found. These are not generic questions — they're built around what was actually suspicious in that resume. Use them in your phone screen or send to the hiring manager.
    5
    Verify visa / government ID (at offer stage)
    When a candidate is moving to offer, use the Verify ID / Visa tab to upload their H1B approval notice, EAD card, Green Card, driver's license, or passport. AI analyzes it for formatting red flags, altered dates, inconsistent fonts, and missing security features. Always confirm legal decisions through official USCIS / E-Verify channels.
    6
    Generate role-specific interview questions
    Use the Interview Questions tab for any role. Paste the JD, choose the experience level and focus. You get four categories: Technical Depth, Real Experience Verification, Proxy/Fraud Detection, and Behavioral. Copy the full set and share with your hiring manager before the interview.
    7
    Copy the report and share
    Every screening result has a Copy Report button that gives you a clean plain-text summary. Paste it into an email to your hiring manager, into your ATS notes, or into a Slack message. It documents your due diligence.
    The 10 fraud signals — what each one catches
    SignalWhat it detects
    JD FabricationResume written to mirror the job description word-for-word rather than reflecting real experience
    Proxy InterviewSkills that don't match claimed years, vague technical depth, someone else likely doing the interviews
    AI / Copied ResumeChatGPT-style language, identical bullet structure throughout, no personality or specificity
    Fake ExperienceTimeline gaps, unverifiable employers, impossible rapid promotions, titles that don't match level
    Visa MismatchSponsorship language inconsistent with claimed status, location vs authorization contradictions
    Job-Hop PatternEvery 4–6 months across many companies, always "contract ended" — proxy farm or performance issues
    Skills TimelineClaims years of experience in technology that didn't exist that long ago
    Location MismatchResume says one location, LinkedIn says another, wants remote — combined signals
    Body Shop / VendorGeneric formatting, coordinator-written summary, same email domain patterns across submissions
    Rate vs ExpSenior engineer asking below-market rate — experience may be fabricated or a large vendor cut is being taken
    Who this is for
    Corporate Recruiters
    Screen candidates before presenting to hiring managers. Save their time and protect your credibility.
    IT Staffing Firms
    Verify vendor-submitted profiles before client submission. Catch body shop patterns early.
    Hiring Managers
    Run a quick screen before investing time in a technical interview. Use the generated questions directly.
    HR / Compliance Teams
    Document due diligence on visa and work authorization verification at the offer stage.
    Important limitations

    VeraHire is a decision-support tool, not a final arbiter. AI analysis should complement — never replace — human judgment and proper verification processes.

    • Document verification findings must be confirmed through E-Verify or USCIS for any legal hiring decision.
    • Do not request government IDs during pre-screening — only at the offer/I-9 stage.
    • A high risk score warrants investigation, not automatic rejection.
    • VeraHire does not store any candidate data. Everything runs in your browser session only.
